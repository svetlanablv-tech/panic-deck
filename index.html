How this works

Press the button and you get one card. Do what it says. Not helping? Draw another. There are no wrong cards.

  • In public: nobody around will notice. Bus, meeting, queue.
  • Needs space: stand up or move a little.
  • Needs a thing: water, pen, phone, tap.

Five suits of 16: Breathe, Ground, Reframe, Move and Land for after the wave has passed.

Panic Deck is a self-help tool. It is not medical advice or treatment. If something feels more serious than panic, or chest pain is new for you, contact your local emergency number or a doctor. For support near you, try findahelpline.com.

No account, no tracking. The only thing saved is your "I'm in public" switch, on this device.
